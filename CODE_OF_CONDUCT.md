# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Discuss behavior and technical
tradeoffs, not personal characteristics. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and the publication of credentials or private account data are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or report
credible security and safety threats. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner. Reports will be handled with discretion and without
retaliation.
